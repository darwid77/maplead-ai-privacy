# Privacy Policy for MapLead AI

Last updated: June 2026

MapLead AI is a Chrome extension developed by Grow Labs.

## Overview

MapLead AI helps users extract publicly visible business information from Google Maps and export it for lead generation purposes.

We are committed to protecting user privacy and being transparent about how information is processed.

## Information We Process

MapLead AI may process publicly visible business information displayed on Google Maps pages selected by the user, including:

* Business Name
* Category
* Address
* Phone Number
* Website
* Rating
* Review Count

This information is extracted only when the user explicitly uses the extension.

## Local Processing

Lead extraction is performed locally within the user's browser.

Extracted lead data is stored locally using Chrome storage and is not transmitted to our servers for lead generation purposes.

## License Validation

MapLead AI offers Free and Pro plans.

To validate Pro licenses and subscription status, the extension communicates with our secure licensing service hosted through Supabase.

Information processed for licensing purposes may include:

* License Key
* License activation status
* Technical identifiers required to verify license validity

This information is used solely for license management and feature activation.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer user data to third parties.

Lead data extracted from Google Maps is not shared with external parties by MapLead AI.

## Data Storage

Lead information remains under the control of the user and is stored locally within the browser unless the user chooses to export it.

License-related information may be stored securely within our licensing system to manage subscriptions and feature access.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used for license validation and service operation.

## Third-Party Services

MapLead AI uses Supabase for license validation and subscription management.

Users may review Supabase's privacy practices at:
https://supabase.com/privacy

## User Control

Users may remove locally stored data at any time by clearing extension data or uninstalling the extension.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
